Method

For each trading period, the supply points (S) and demand points (D) are ordered by SeqNr and joined with straight lines, as on the Nord Pool curve charts. Demand volume falls and supply volume rises as the price goes up, so the excess demand D(p) − S(p) decreases with price. The clearing price is the price at which it reaches zero:

  • if the curves cross on a sloped segment, the price is interpolated linearly between the neighbouring curve points;
  • if they cross on a flat (horizontal) segment, the price is that segment's price;
  • if they overlap along a vertical segment, the price range is ambiguous and the midpoint is used (flagged in the table).

Prices are rounded to two decimals for display; the downloaded CSV keeps four.

Data stays in this browser tab and is discarded when you close or reload it.
